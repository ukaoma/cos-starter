Adoption has a zip code

The US Census Bureau asks businesses every two weeks whether they use AI. Eurostat asks European enterprises with 10 or more staff once a year. The frames differ, so compare trajectories, not decimal points.

In November 2025 Census broadened its question from AI “in producing goods or services” to AI in “any of its business functions,” and the national share stepped from 10% to 17%. It has climbed since, to 24.2% in the release of November 5, 2026. The chart marks that break.

Europe · Eurostat, 2025, enterprises 10+, still the latest wave

Size moves the number as much as place: in Europe, 55% of enterprises with 250 or more staff used AI in 2025, against 17% of those with 10 to 49. Intensity is another map again: on Anthropic's Economic Index, Washington DC uses Claude at 3.8× its population share.

The numbers, sourced

Every figure on this page, with its tier: official statistics (T1), published surveys (T2), company and industry figures (T3), press estimates (T4).

How to read all of this honestly

  • Never sum provider users. Only surveys estimate unduplicated people.
  • Survey wording drives the number. “Ever used” (61%), “weekly” (34%) and “used ChatGPT” (44%) are different questions. Where a survey changed its question (Pew in 2026, Census in November 2025) the chart marks the break.
  • Company figures are unaudited and usually disclosed at launches and earnings. All are dated to the disclosure.
  • The dot grid is a model. The weekly tier is a floor (ChatGPT alone); “tried it” extrapolates surveyed ratios to online adults; paying and building are order-of-magnitude seat counts.

Questions people actually ask

How many people use ChatGPT?

OpenAI reported 1.2 billion weekly users at DevDay in September 2026, up from 800 million in October 2025. That is the largest disclosed figure for any AI product. In the US, 44% of adults told Pew in February 2026 they have used it, up from 18% in 2023.

What share of people use generative AI?

In the six countries the Reuters Institute tracks, 61% have tried it and 34% use it weekly (2025). Across 21 countries, Google and Ipsos found 48% used AI in the past year. Globally, ChatGPT alone reaches about one human in seven in a given week.

What share of businesses use AI?

About 24% of US firms (Census BTOS, release of November 5, 2026, on a question broadened in November 2025; on the original question it rose from under 4% in late 2023 to 10% in September 2025) and 20% of EU enterprises (Eurostat 2025), led by Denmark at 42%. Enterprise surveys of larger firms run much higher. Stanford HAI's broad measure hits 88%.

Which US state uses AI most?

Across the six Census releases from August to November 2026, Washington DC leads at 34.6% of businesses, then Utah (31.5%) and Colorado (28.8%); West Virginia is lowest at 13.6%, against a national average of 23.7%. On per-capita usage intensity of Claude, DC is in a league of its own at 3.8× its population share.

Can you add ChatGPT and Gemini users for a total?

No. The counts overlap (same people, several tools), they are self-reported, and they mix incompatible metrics: weekly vs monthly users vs downloads vs seats. That is why this page anchors its index on population surveys and treats provider numbers as dated milestones.
