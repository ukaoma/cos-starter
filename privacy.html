Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

The short version: COS Glasses processes everything locally on your machine. We don't collect, store, or transmit your data. Your queries, files, and voice recordings never leave your local network.

What COS Glasses Does

COS Glasses is an AI heads-up display application for Even G2 smart glasses. It connects your glasses to a server running on your own computer, enabling voice and text queries routed to Claude (Anthropic's AI) through your own account.

Data We Collect

None. COS Glasses has no backend servers, no analytics, no telemetry, and no user accounts. The application runs entirely on your local machine.

How Your Data Flows

  • Voice queries: Audio is recorded on your glasses, sent to your local server over your local network, and transcribed using your own OpenAI API key (if configured). Audio is never stored.
  • Text queries: Sent from the companion app to your local server. Routed to Claude using your own Claude CLI session or Anthropic API key.
  • File browsing: The companion app reads directories on your machine via the local server. Files are sandboxed to the project directory you specify.
  • Conversation history: Stored in a JSON file on your local machine. Not transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

COS Glasses connects to third-party services only through your own accounts and API keys:

  • Anthropic (Claude): AI queries are processed through your Claude CLI subscription or your own Anthropic API key. Subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI (Whisper): Voice transcription, if enabled, uses your own OpenAI API key. Subject to OpenAI's privacy policy.

We have no access to your accounts or API keys. These are stored in a local .env file on your machine.

Network Permissions

The Even Hub app requests network permissions to connect to:

  • localhost / 127.0.0.1 — your local server
  • Your local network IP — for glasses-to-server communication
  • evenhub.evenrealities.com — Even Hub platform (required by Even SDK)

Children's Privacy

COS Glasses is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page. Since we collect no data, changes are unlikely.
