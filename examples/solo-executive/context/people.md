## Key Stakeholders

### David Kim — CEO & Co-founder

- **What he cares about:** ARR growth, v3 ship date, Series B readiness
- **Communication style:** Direct, impatient with ambiguity. Wants the bottom line first, then supporting data if he asks.
- **Prep for every interaction:** Have v3 status, top risk, and one decision you need from him ready.
- **Pattern:** Tends to micromanage when anxious. If he starts asking for daily updates, something spooked him — find out what.

### Rachel Stein — VP Engineering

- **What she cares about:** Team health, technical debt, sustainable pace
- **Communication style:** Collaborative, evidence-based. Open to trade-offs if you frame them well.
- **Working relationship:** Strong. We disagree constructively. She'll push back on scope creep — that's a feature.
- **Watch for:** When she says "my team is stretched," believe her. She has 14 engineers across 3 squads.

### Omar Hassan — Head of Sales

- **What he cares about:** Pipeline, close rate, competitive features
- **Communication style:** Enthusiastic, deal-focused. Every customer conversation becomes a feature request.
- **How to manage:** Listen, log, don't commit. "That's great signal, let me evaluate against the roadmap." Never say yes in the moment.
- **Useful for:** Competitive intelligence. He hears what prospects say about competitors daily.

### Lisa Tran — Head of Customer Success

- **What she cares about:** Retention, NPS, customer health scores
- **Communication style:** Data-driven, calm, precise. If she says something is a problem, it's already been a problem for 2 weeks.
- **Monthly sync:** First Thursday of each month, 1pm. She brings churn risk data.
- **Most valuable for:** Early warning on product issues. Customers tell CS things they don't put in support tickets.
