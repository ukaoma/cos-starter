Queue-Based Architecture

Meeting sync enqueues instantly (~0ms). Graph processing runs separately on a schedule. Zero impact on sync pipeline speed.

Budget Controlled

Hard daily call cap with audit logging. Budget exhaustion defers items to next day, never corrupts data. All calls logged.

Entity Resolution

Entities and relationships extracted automatically during ingestion. Names, companies, and decisions merge across sources without manual mapping.
